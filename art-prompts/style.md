# Shared style block

Prepend this to every prompt, or attach an existing image as a style reference.
It is a description of the art already in `src/images/`, not a new direction.

> Soft children's-storybook illustration in coloured pencil and light
> watercolour. Warm, gentle, hand-drawn feel with visible paper grain. Soft
> rounded forms, delicate outlines rather than heavy black ink, muted saturation.
> Light is warm and glowing — lanterns, candlelight, magical sparkles drifting in
> the air — with cool teal shadows for contrast. Tender and inviting, a bedtime
> story rather than a cartoon. No harsh contrast, no digital gloss, no 3D render.

## Palette

New art should sit inside this range. The stylesheet is built from it.

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Warm near-black, ink and outlines | `#2A1F16` |
| Parchment, paper, highlights | `#F6EBD7` |
| Honey — lantern light, glow | `#F0B457` |
| Sage — Braillinda's dress, foliage | `#93AC77` |
| Dusk teal — shadow, sky, interiors | `#2E4A4C` |
| Poppy red — amapolas, accents | `#C0392B` |
| Muted violet — twilight sky | `#9385B6` |

## Avoid

Photorealism · 3D render · anime · thick black comic outlines · neon or
oversaturated colour · harsh shadows · lens flare · text or lettering anywhere in
the image · watermarks · modern clothing or objects · visible braille text that
might be wrong (the app renders real braille itself — never let the model draw it)
