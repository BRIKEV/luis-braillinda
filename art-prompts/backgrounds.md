# Backgrounds

Three locations cover the story as transcribed in `src/data/content.ts`.
All **2048×1152 (16:9)**, no characters, no text.

Prepend `style.md` to each prompt.

---

## 1. `bg-meadow` — the autumn poppy meadow

**Covers roughly entries 1–12:** Braillinda's beautiful wings, the fairies
reading the future on poppy petals, the grandmother asking for help blowing the
seeds, and the discovery — her fingertips finding a "corona de puntos", a crown
of dots, around each seed capsule. This is the emotional heart of the story and
right now it renders in a classroom.

Generate this one first and iterate until the style is right.

> An autumn meadow at golden hour, seen from low down among the flowers. Tall
> red poppies with dark seed capsules, their crowns catching the light. Large
> red-and-white spotted mushrooms with soft rounded caps. A gnarled tree trunk
> with a small hollow at the left edge. Dry golden grasses, drifting dandelion
> seeds and tiny glowing motes in the air. Warm low sunlight from the right, long
> soft shadows, a hazy pale-gold and dusty-violet sky. Empty in the middle
> ground so characters can stand there. Peaceful, nostalgic, slightly magical.

**Notes:** the red-and-white mushrooms and the poppies both come from the source
book's own illustrations. The seed capsules matter — they are what she touches.

## 2. `bg-paris` — outside the Institute, at night

**Covers entries 13–14:** the journey to Paris, "el instituto de ciegos de la
calle de Saint-Victor". Optional, but it marks the story's turn from the fairy
world to the human one.

> A narrow cobbled Paris street on a cold autumn night, early nineteenth century.
> A tall pale stone building with a heavy wooden door and rows of tall windows,
> two or three of them glowing warm amber from within. A single wrought-iron
> street lantern casting a pool of honey light on wet cobbles. Bare branches
> against a deep teal-violet sky with a scatter of stars and a little mist.
> Quiet, hushed, expectant — somewhere a small traveller is arriving. Empty
> foreground.

## 3. `bg-workshop` — the Institute workroom

**Covers entries 14 onward:** where Luis is asleep at his work table, and where
the two of them invent the letters. This replaces the existing square
`background.png` — same room, correct aspect ratio.

> The interior of an old stone institute workroom at dusk. Vaulted arched
> ceilings in warm pale stone, tall bookshelves crammed with worn books, a large
> dark green chalkboard on the back wall, sturdy wooden work tables and stools. A
> tall arched window on the right looking out on a misty teal twilight garden.
> Hanging brass lanterns and a candle giving warm amber light, tiny golden motes
> drifting in the air. One open book on a table glowing gently from its pages.
> Cosy, scholarly, quietly magical. Empty in the centre foreground so characters
> can stand there.

**Note:** the existing background has a chalkboard with dots drawn on it. Leave
the chalkboard **blank** — the app renders real braille, and hand-drawn dots risk
being wrong in a story that teaches the code.
